# Privacy Policy for UrbanSetu Mobile Application

**Last Updated:** May 27, 2026

UrbanSetu ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") distributed via our official channels.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
When you register or use the App, we may collect:
* **Account Credentials:** Username, email address, password, phone number.
* **Profile Information:** First name, last name, avatar image, bio.
* **Verification Data:** Identity documentation (for verified owners/admins to guarantee platform security).

### B. Device Permissions
To provide real-time real estate services, the App may request access to:
* **Location Services:** To locate nearby properties, map routes, and calculate proximity to local services. You can opt-out by disabling location services in device settings.
* **Camera / Media Library:** To allow owners and agents to take pictures/videos of properties and upload them directly.
* **Network & Storage:** To cache listings data, map assets, and keep communication channels open.

### C. Automatic Data Collection
* **Log Data:** IP address, device type, operating system, app crashes, and usage analytics.
* **Cookies & Sessions:** To maintain your authenticated state across app sessions securely.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:
* **Facilitate Property Management:** List, verify, and search properties.
* **Enable Communications:** Route appointments, messages, and video calls between buyers and owners/agents.
* **Ensure Security:** Real-time Sentinel AI checks scan for fraudulent activities to prevent scams.
* **Improve the App:** Analyze usage trends to optimize user experience and fix system bugs.

---

## 3. Storage and Data Security

* **Data Encryption:** All transmission between the App and our backend APIs is encrypted using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS) protocols. Data at rest is encrypted in our secure databases.
* **Data Retention:** We retain your personal data only as long as your account is active or needed to provide services. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time via App settings.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with:
* **Service Providers:** Cloud storage (AWS S3, Cloudinary) to host uploaded images, and communication networks (Socket.io/TURN) to facilitate calls.
* **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, to protect our rights, or to enforce platform safety policies.
